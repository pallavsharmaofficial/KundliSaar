The app stays free either way

KundliSaar has no advertising, no subscription and nothing to buy inside it. It does not collect your data, so there is nothing to sell about you either. That is deliberate, and it is how it will stay.

It does take time. The ephemeris alone was fitted and checked against NASA's own figures so the charts hold to an arc-second, and every reading is written out in two languages by hand. If the app has been useful to you, you can send something towards that.

Other ways that cost nothing
