Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version

The KundliSaar app collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Everything the app computes, it computes on your own device.

One exception, and only if you use it: the separate web page “आपका अच्छा समय कब आएगा?” (reached from our printed flyers) does store what you type into it, with your consent, so that our team can send you your kundli's password and talk to you. Exactly what it keeps is below. The rest of this page is about the app.

What stays on your device

Your name, date, time and place of birth, the charts made from them, and your settings are written to this device's own storage — app storage on Android and iOS, the browser's local storage on the web. They are never uploaded, and we cannot read them. Deleting a kundli in the app, or clearing the app's data, removes them.

Microphone, camera and photos

All three are optional and every screen works without them.

  • Microphone. Only while you hold the mic button. Your speech is turned into text by the recogniser already built into your device or browser, and the text goes straight into the app. KundliSaar does not record, store or transmit audio.
  • Camera and photos. Only on the hastrekha screen, and only after you tap to open the camera (your phone or browser asks first). The app looks at your palm through the camera, on the device itself, with models that are bundled inside the phone app, and takes the picture when your hand is steady. The frames are looked at and dropped. The one picture the reading is made from is held in memory while you read it and is gone when you leave the screen; it is never saved, never sent anywhere, and never used to identify anyone. If you choose to pick a photo of your palm to trace over, it is held in memory in the same way. In a browser it works the same way, inside the page: the first time you open the camera the page fetches the hand-finding files (about 17 MB: Google's MediaPipe hand models and the LiteRT WebAssembly runtime, both Apache 2.0) from this same site and nowhere else, and runs them on your own device. Your picture is never sent to those files or anywhere else.
  • Reading aloud uses the text-to-speech voice already on your device.

What those built-in services do with what they hear is governed by your device maker's or browser's own privacy policy, not ours.

The network

The app does not call any API. The place list, the time zone database and the planetary tables are bundled inside it, which is why it works with the radio off. The web version is downloaded from GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub sees the request for the page itself. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children. A parent casting a chart for a newborn is doing it on their own device, and nothing leaves it.

The “अच्छा समय” web page — what it stores

This applies only to the page at samay/, not to the app. You are asked to agree before anything is sent.

  • What you type: your name, mobile number, date, time and place of birth, and whether the time is approximate.
  • The kundli made from it: your rashi, lagna, nakshatra, the running dasha, the planets' positions and the short answer the page showed you. The full kundli PDF is made in your browser and is not uploaded.
  • Technical details: your IP address, your browser's description (user agent), the page that sent you, and which flyer or post you came from (the src in the link). Also whether you opened the “basis” section, downloaded the PDF, or tapped call or WhatsApp.
  • A password and a code: each visitor's PDF gets its own password, kept with your record, and a code such as KS-123456 so our team can find you when you call.

Why: to give you the password to your kundli, to contact you on the number you gave about your kundli and our paid reports or consultations, to stop abuse (the IP address limits how many entries one network can make), and to learn which of our flyers work. We do not sell or give your details to anyone else, and we do not use them to make decisions about you.

Where: in a database run for us by Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India. Only our own staff can read it, after signing in; each staff member sees what their role allows, and every password they reveal is logged.

How long: up to 24 months after your last contact with us, then deleted. You can ask us sooner.

Your rights: under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or erase your details, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Call or WhatsApp 99101 44125 with your code or mobile number. Erasing removes your name, number, birth details, IP and browser details; only a count that someone came from a given flyer remains.

Children: the page is meant for adults. A parent may fill it in for a child, and agrees on the child's behalf.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes — and the intention is that it does not — the new policy will be published here and in the app before the change takes effect. The app is open source: you can read exactly what it does at github.com/pallavsharmaofficial/KundliSaar and raise anything at its issue tracker.
