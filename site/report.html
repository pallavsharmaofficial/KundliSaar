Why the numbers can be trusted

Planetary positions come from our own mathematics, fitted to NASA JPL's public-domain DE440s ephemeris and checked automatically against it: under two arc-seconds for the Sun, under four for the planets, across 1900 to 2070. Gandhi's published chart reproduces sign for sign. The same engine runs inside the free app, so you can check any figure in the report yourself.

What the report will not do: no predictions about death, illness, pregnancy, exam results, court cases or investments. A dosha is given with its cancellation, because a dosha is a configuration and not a verdict on a person.
