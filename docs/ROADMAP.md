# Roadmap

## Done (v0.1)

- Own ephemeris in pure Dart, fitted to JPL DE440s, arc-second class 1900–2070.
- Lagna chart in three styles, all sixteen divisional charts, planet detail with
  dignity, retrogression and combustion.
- Vimshottari to three levels and the Yogini cycle.
- Yogas and doshas that name the rule that fired, with classical cancellations.
- Full daily panchang with timings, choghadiya and horas for any date and place.
- Ashtakoota matching with every koota explained.
- Ask: questions answered from the chart in Hindi and English, with the chart
  factors shown.
- Learn: grahas, rashis and nakshatras with deity, symbol and lord.
- Hindi and English throughout, offline, no account.

## Next

1. **Strengths.** Shadbala with its six components, ashtakavarga with
   prastara and sarvashtakavarga marked on the chart.
2. **Transits.** Gochar view with Saturn and Jupiter tracks, and a dasha and
   transit timeline on one axis.
3. **Muhurta finder.** Search a date range for griha pravesh, vehicle purchase,
   naming, travel or a business start.
4. **Voice.** Read any reading aloud, and ask questions by speaking them.
5. **Mythology and illustration.** The painted figures for nine grahas, twelve
   rashis and 27 nakshatras, with the Puranic account for each.
6. **Optional on-device model.** Gemma 3 1B behind the same answer interface,
   grounded in the computed chart and the rule base, downloaded only on request.
7. **Varshphal**, Ashtottari and Chara dashas, East Indian chart style.
8. **Android and iOS store builds**, once the web beta has been through real
   charts.
