# Roadmap

## Done (v0.2)

- **Shadbala** with all six strengths in rupas, and **ashtakavarga** with every
  bhinnashtakavarga and the sarvashtakavarga.
- **Gochar** from the natal Moon, with bindus, transit aspects, Sade Sati phase
  dates and the next Jupiter and Saturn returns.
- **Muhurta finder** across a date range for ten activities, scoring every
  choghadiya slot and showing the reasons.
- **Varshphal**: solar return, Muntha, and the year lord chosen by shadbala.
- **Festival and vrat calendar**, computed per place, with the observance-time
  rules (pradosh, nishita, midday) and Bhadra.
- **Hastrekha**: guided palm tracing with the classical line and mount rules,
  cross-read against the kundli.
- **Ank Jyotish**, **Prashna**, **daily reading**, **namkaran**, and
  **remedies** chosen from the chart.
- **Voice**: ask by speaking, hear the answer read back, in both languages.
- A **swamiji avatar** and **27 nakshatra loaders**, both drawn in code.

## Done (v0.1)

- Own ephemeris in pure Dart, fitted to JPL DE440s, arc-second class 1900–2070.
- Lagna chart in three styles, all sixteen divisional charts, planet detail with
  dignity, retrogression and combustion.
- Vimshottari to three levels and the Yogini cycle.
- Yogas and doshas that name the rule that fired, with classical cancellations.
- Full daily panchang with timings, choghadiya and horas for any date and place.
- Ashtakoota matching with every koota explained.
- Ask: questions answered from the chart in Hindi and English, with the chart
  factors shown.
- Learn: grahas, rashis and nakshatras with deity, symbol and lord.
- Hindi and English throughout, offline, no account.

## Next

1. **Mythology and illustration.** The painted figures for nine grahas, twelve
   rashis and 27 nakshatras, with the Puranic account for each.
2. **Optional on-device model.** Gemma 3 1B behind the same answer interface,
   grounded in the computed chart and the rule base, downloaded only on request.
3. **Ashtakavarga on the chart face**, prastara tables, and a dasha and transit
   timeline on one axis.
4. **Ashtottari and Chara dashas**, and the East Indian chart style.
5. **Sadhana tracker** for the remedies, with a daily count kept on the device.
6. **Android and iOS store builds**, once the web beta has been through real
   charts.
