# Selling the kundli report

The product is a 25 to 40 page PDF kundli, generated from the same engine the
app uses, delivered by hand for now.

## The price, and why

| Who | Report | Price |
|---|---|---|
| AstroTalk | Accurate Kundli | 1,599, on sale at 799 |
| AstroTalk | Bhagya Kundli | 3,999, on sale at 999 |
| AstroSage | Brihat Kundli, 250 pages | 2,100, on sale at 996 |
| **Ours** | **25 to 40 pages, both languages** | **299** |

Do not invent a struck-through "regular price" we never charged. The honest
line is that comparable reports sell for 799 to 1,599, which is true, checkable,
and does the same work without the lie.

Three hundred is deliberately low. The first twenty sales are not for the money;
they are for the testimonials, the questions that show what the report is
missing, and the proof that people will pay at all.

## The order flow, today

1. They read the page and look at the sample.
2. They pay 299 by UPI to `pallavsharma353-1@okaxis`.
3. They send name, date, exact time and town of birth, plus the payment
   screenshot, on WhatsApp or by email.
4. The report is generated and sent back, same day.

It is manual, and that is fine for the first twenty. Automate it when the
manual version is the bottleneck, not before.

## Who to tell first, tonight

In this order, because this is the order that converts.

1. **Family and close friends on WhatsApp.** Not a broadcast. Individual
   messages. Most will not buy and that is fine; two or three will, and they
   will tell someone.
2. **The twelve app testers.** They already installed your app. Offer them the
   report free in exchange for one honest paragraph about it. Those paragraphs
   are what sell the next fifty.
3. **Family WhatsApp groups**, once two people outside your house have paid and
   said it was good. Not before: a group is one shot.
4. **Local astrologers.** They charge for consultations, not for chart
   calculation, and doing the maths by hand costs them an hour per client. A
   report at wholesale saves them that hour. This is the channel with the
   highest ceiling and nobody uses it.

## What not to do

- Do not post the link in groups you are not part of. It gets you blocked and
  teaches people your name means spam.
- Do not promise what the report does not do. No death dates, no illness, no
  exam results, no court cases, no stock tips. The refusal is in the report and
  it should stay in the sales pitch too; people trust it more, not less.
- Do not buy reviews, installs or traffic. It risks the Play account you are
  three weeks from launching on, and the account is worth more than the sales.
