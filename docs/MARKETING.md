# Getting the first thousand users, and the first hundred sales

Written for a standing start: no audience, no budget, no list. Everything here
is free or nearly so. Paid advertising is at the end, where it belongs, because
spending money to send strangers to an unproven page wastes the money and
teaches you nothing.

## The one number that matters first

Not installs. **Whether anyone pays 299 rupees.** Twenty paid reports tell you
more than two thousand free installs, because a payment is the only honest
signal that the thing is worth something. Chase that first.

## Week one, in order

**Day 1, tonight.** Individual WhatsApp messages to family and close friends.
Not a broadcast list, not a group. Twenty messages, written one at a time. Three
or four will buy. That is the proof.

**Day 1.** Offer the full report free to the twelve app testers in exchange for
one honest paragraph. Those paragraphs become the testimonials that sell
everything after this, and you cannot buy them later.

**Day 2 to 3.** The report goes on the site, the sample is public, and the free
app links to it. Anyone who likes the app has somewhere to spend money.

**Day 3 onwards.** Local astrologers, in person. This is the channel nobody
uses and it has the highest ceiling. An astrologer charges for the reading, not
the arithmetic, and the arithmetic costs them an hour a client. Offer the
report at 99 wholesale. One astrologer doing five clients a week is more
reliable income than a hundred strangers.

**Day 4 to 7.** Family and community WhatsApp groups, but only once two people
outside your house have paid and said it was good. A group is one shot. Spend
it when you have a testimonial, not before.

## The content engine, which is already built

Forty-eight reference pages generated from the app's own engine: twenty-seven
nakshatras, twelve rashis, nine grahas, plus a daily panchang page rebuilt by a
scheduled job. This is not filler. Someone searching **rohini nakshatra** or
**ashlesha nakshatra pada** is looking for exactly that page, and there are tens
of thousands of those searches a month in India.

That traffic does three things: it sells reports, it installs the app, and it
is what makes AdSense approvable. A three-page site gets rejected as thin
content; a fifty-page reference site does not.

Expect nothing for three weeks, then a slow climb. Search is the only channel
here that compounds.

## App store optimisation, already done

The listing title carries both the brand and the two words people search:
`KundliSaar: Kundli & Panchang`. The Hindi listing exists, which most
competitors skip, and Play weights an exact-language listing heavily for Hindi
locale users. Screenshots lead with the chart, because that is what a person
scanning the store is checking for.

The lever you still hold: **ratings**. Ask every tester, and every report buyer,
for a rating. Play shows apps with ratings and buries apps without them. Twenty
ratings is the difference between invisible and findable.

## Social, in the order worth doing

1. **WhatsApp Status.** Free, and your contacts already trust you. One image of
   a real chart, one line, the link. Post it the day the report goes live.
2. **Instagram Reels.** The format that works for this niche is thirty seconds:
   a question on screen, the app answering it, the answer explained. Not a
   promo. People follow explanation, not advertisement.
3. **YouTube Shorts.** Same clips, different audience, no extra work.
4. **Reddit** r/vedicastrology and r/IndianAstrology. Read the rules first, post
   as a person who built something and not as a seller, and let people ask. A
   free, open-source, ad-free kundli app is genuinely interesting to that
   audience. Anything that smells like marketing is removed in minutes.
5. **Quora**, Hindi and English. Answer the question properly and mention the
   tool at the end. Answers rank in Google for years.

## What to say, and what not to

The honest positioning is the strongest one available, and it happens to be
true: computed on the device, nothing uploaded, no account, no advertising,
no fear, and it refuses to predict death, illness or exam results. Every
competitor does the opposite. Say it plainly.

Never claim the app predicts the future. Never put a struck-through price on
something that never sold at it. Never buy reviews, installs or traffic: it
risks the Play account you are three weeks from launching on, and the account
is worth more than any sale it would bring.

## Paid advertising, when you have something proven

Do not spend a rupee until twenty people have paid 299 unprompted. When they
have:

- **Google Search ads** on `kundli report`, `janam kundli pdf`, `kundali milan`,
  Hindi and English. Start at 200 a day. The intent is already there; you are
  paying to be seen by someone who has decided to buy.
- **Meta ads** are cheaper per click and worse per rupee for this, because the
  intent is not there. Use them only to retarget people who saw the report page
  and left.
- The number to watch is cost per paid report, not cost per click. Below 100
  rupees, spend more. Above 250, stop and fix the page.

## What is being measured

Put nothing invasive on the site. A privacy-respecting counter is enough:
how many saw the report page, how many pressed Pay. If the first number is
large and the second is not, the page is wrong. If both are small, nobody is
arriving, and the problem is upstream.
