Title: I built a free, open-source Vedic astrology app that computes
everything on-device

Body: I got frustrated that every kundli app either wants an account, shows
ads, or charges to tell you that you have a dosha. So I wrote the ephemeris
myself — a Poisson series fitted to JPL DE440s, holding under two arc-seconds
for the Sun across 1900-2070 — and built the app on top.

Charts in three styles, all sixteen vargas, shadbala, ashtakavarga, Vimshottari
to three levels, full panchang with timings, Ashtakoota matching. Hindi and
English. No account, nothing uploaded, no ads, MIT licensed.

It refuses questions about death, illness, pregnancy and exams, which I know
costs it users, but I would rather it did.

Happy to answer anything about the maths. Source and web version in the
comments.
