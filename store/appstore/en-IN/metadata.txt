REVIEW NOTES
The app has no account and no server. Everything is computed on the device from
bundled data. The microphone is used only while the user holds the mic button on
the Ask screen, and the camera only if the user chooses to photograph their own
palm on the Hastrekha screen; both are optional and every screen works without
them. The app is free with no purchases.
